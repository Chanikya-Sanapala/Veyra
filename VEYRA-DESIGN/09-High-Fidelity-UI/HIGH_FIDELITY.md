## 2. High-Fidelity Visual Rules & Constraints
1. **Light-First Enterprise Aesthetic:** White (`#FFFFFF`) canvas and Slate-50 (`#F8FAFC`) card surfaces create high contrast and legibility, eliminating legacy CHANIX dark supercar neon aesthetics.
2. **Explainable Intelligence First:** Score badges display composite ratings (`94% Match`) alongside explicit sub-score bars and verified evidence (`✓ React`).
3. **Human Agency Control:** Visual hierarchy elevates human decision buttons (`[ Shortlist ]`, `[ Move to Interview ]`, `[ Hire ]`), rendering AI assessments strictly as decision-support signals.

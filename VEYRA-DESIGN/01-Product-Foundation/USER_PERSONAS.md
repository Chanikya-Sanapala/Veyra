# VEYRA — User Personas

---

## Persona 1: Recruiter / Hiring Manager

### Profile Summary
- **Name:** Sarah Jenkins
- **Role:** Lead Technical Recruiter / Talent Acquisition Specialist
- **Experience:** 6+ years in technical hiring
- **Environment:** High-volume hiring environment managing 15-20 active open job requisitions simultaneously.

### Goals
- Identify top-tier technical talent quickly from large applicant pools.
- Reduce time-to-hire without compromising candidate quality.
- Access clear, evidence-backed justifications for candidate match scores before scheduling interviews.
- Standardize technical screening and preliminary behavioral assessments.
- Track candidate status seamlessly through intuitive pipeline stages.

### Problems & Frustrations
- Overwhelmed by hundreds of unqualified or keyword-stuffed resumes.
- Disjointed tools for resume parsing, video interviewing, applicant tracking, and candidate scoring.
- Subjective candidate evaluation across different team interviewers.
- High candidate ghosting and lengthy manual scheduling workflows.

### Main Actions in VEYRA
- Create and configure job requisitions with specific technical requirements.
- Review automatically parsed candidate applications and ranked talent lists.
- Analyze transparent Resume Match Score breakdowns (Skills, Experience, Projects, Education).
- Trigger AI interview invitations for shortlisted applicants.
- Review AI-generated interview evaluation reports and transcript highlights.
- Move candidates through pipeline stages (Applied → Matched → Interviewed → Shortlisted → Hired).

### Information Required
- Overall Match Score % and granular breakdown scores.
- Matched skills vs missing skill gaps.
- Key project highlights and relevance to job description.
- AI Interview score, transcript summary, and competency evaluations.

### Key VEYRA Features
- Recruiter Workspace & Candidate Ranking.
- Instant Resume-to-Job Matching Engine.
- Skill Gap & Match Breakdown Views.
- Automated AI Interview Evaluation Reports.
- Pipeline Management & Hiring Analytics Dashboard.

### Success Criteria
- 50% reduction in resume screening time.
- Increased candidate pass rate in final hiring manager interviews.
- Clear data trail backing every shortlist recommendation.

## Persona 2: Candidate / Job Seeker

### Profile Summary
- **Name:** Alex Rivera
- **Role:** Senior Full-Stack Engineer / Active Career Builder
- **Experience:** 4 years in software engineering
- **Environment:** Seeking growth opportunities in modern tech stacks.

### Goals
- Discover job openings that genuinely match existing technical skills and experience.
- Understand how candidate profile and resume rate against specific job descriptions.
- Receive transparent feedback on missing skills and concrete action items for profile enhancement.
- Demonstrate technical and problem-solving skills through dynamic AI interview workouts.
- Track job application progress transparently.

### Problems & Frustrations
- "Black hole" job applications with zero feedback or response.
- ATS keyword filters rejecting strong candidates due to resume formatting.
- Lack of clarity on why a application was rejected or what skills are missing.
- Repetitive, manual application form fill-outs across multiple portals.

### Main Actions in VEYRA
- Create candidate profile and upload resume (PDF/DOCX).
- Explore recommended jobs curated by VEYRA's matching algorithm.
- Review personal Resume Match Scores and skill gap suggestions for targeted jobs.
- Complete preliminary AI technical and behavioral interview sessions.
- Monitor application statuses across candidate dashboard.
- Update profile, projects, and skills to continuously boost match scores.

### Information Required
- Personalized Resume Match Score for target roles.
- Granular breakdown showing strengths and missing skill requirements.
- Job requirements, compensation, location, and application deadline details.
- Application status updates and interview performance ratings.

### Key VEYRA Features
- Candidate Workspace & Profile Builder.
- Automated Resume Parsing & Skill Extraction.
- Personalized Job Match & Skill Gap Breakdown.
- AI Interview Practice & Workout Module.
- Application Tracker & Notification Hub.

### Success Criteria
- High confidence in application relevance before submitting.
- Actionable insight into skill gaps for targeted roles.
- Faster response and invitation rates from potential employers.

# VEYRA — Product Brief

## Product Overview
- **Product Name:** VEYRA
- **Category:** AI-powered Talent Intelligence Platform
- **Tagline:** See potential. Hire with confidence.
- **Parent Platform / Origin:** Evolved from CHANIX Recruitment Application

---

## Primary User Groups

### 1. Recruiters & Hiring Managers
- Talent Acquisition Lead
- Senior Recruiter
- Hiring Manager / Team Lead

### 2. Candidates & Job Seekers
- Active Job Seekers
- Passive Talent / Career Builders
- Recent Graduates & Professionals

---

## The Problem

### For Recruiters
- **Volume Overload:** Recruiters receive hundreds of resumes per position, making manual screening slow, subjective, and prone to fatigue.
- **Lack of Objective Insights:** Traditional resume screening relies on surface-level keyword matching rather than understanding core skills, potential, and project relevance.
- **Inconsistent Evaluation:** Interview processes and candidate evaluations lack standardization, leading to cognitive bias and inefficient hiring cycles.

### For Candidates
- **Black Box Applications:** Candidates apply to jobs with zero visibility into how their skills, experience, and profile match requirement expectations.
- **Unclear Skill Gaps:** Job seekers lack actionable feedback on why they were rejected or what skills/experiences they need to bridge to qualify for target roles.
- **Superficial Matching:** Static keyword matching fails to highlight candidate accomplishments, projects, and domain strength.

---

## The VEYRA Solution

VEYRA transforms recruitment into an objective, data-rich intelligence workflow:

1. **Deep Resume Parsing & Intelligence:** Automatically extracts skills, experience, education, and project highlights from uploaded resume documents.
2. **Multi-Dimensional Resume-to-Job Matching:** Computes a transparent **Resume Match Score** evaluating Skill Fit, Experience Fit, Project Relevance, Education Alignment, and Role Fit.
3. **Actionable Skill Gap Analysis:** Explicitly highlights missing skills and provides clear suggestions for candidate improvement.
4. **Structured AI-Powered Interviews:** Conducts interactive technical and behavioral AI interview sessions with real-time response capture and dynamic scoring.
5. **Unified Candidate Intelligence:** Aggregates match metrics, skill verification, project depth, and AI interview insights into a single actionable Recruiter Decision View.
6. **Accelerated Hiring Workflows:** Empowers recruiters to rank, shortlist, compare, and make data-driven hiring decisions with confidence.

# VEYRA — Core Product Features

---

## 01. Resume Intelligence

VEYRA automatically extracts structured insights from candidate documents:
- **Resume Upload:** Multi-format file ingestion supporting PDF and DOCX files.
- **Resume Parsing:** Automatic text extraction and section segmentation.
- **Skills Extraction:** Identification of technical languages, frameworks, tools, and domain methodologies.
- **Experience Extraction:** Chronological timeline mapping, title history, tenure calculation, and role responsibilities.
- **Education Extraction:** Degree verification, field of study, institution identification, and graduation timelines.
- **Project Extraction:** Key project highlights, tech stack tags, repository links, and verifiable accomplishments.

---

## 02. Resume Matching (Core Engine)

Algorithmic alignment of candidate resumes against job requisitions:
- **Resume-to-Job Matching:** Semantic vector similarity, TF-IDF vectorization, and fuzzy term matching.
- **Resume Match Score:** Multi-factor percentage calculation rating total suitability.
- **Skill Match Component:** Quantitative comparison of required skills vs extracted candidate skills.
- **Experience Match Component:** Assessment of years of relevant experience vs job requirements.
- **Education Relevance:** Degree level and field of study alignment with job criteria.
- **Project Relevance:** Match score evaluating project technologies and domain overlap.
- **Role Relevance:** Semantic alignment of past job titles with the target role.
- **Skill Gap Analysis:** Explicit listing of missing mandatory and preferred skills.
- **Match Explanation & Suggestions:** Natural language bullet points guiding candidate profile optimization and recruiter evaluation.

---

## 03. AI Interviews

Automated, interactive assessment workflows:
- **AI-Generated Questions:** Dynamic generation of technical and behavioral questions tailored to job requirements.
- **Technical Interviews:** Code understanding, architectural logic, and skill verification scenarios.
- **Behavioral Interviews:** Situation-Behavior-Outcome (STAR method) evaluations and cultural indicator questions.
- **Candidate Responses:** Real-time speech-to-text / text response capture with audio recording option.
- **AI Evaluation:** Automated response scoring against model answers and criteria benchmarks.
- **Interview Scoring & Analytics:** Granular candidate performance ratings and competency breakdown.

---

## 04. Candidate Intelligence

Unified composite dashboard combining multiple intelligence signals:
- Aggregates **Resume Match Score** + **Skills Graph** + **Experience Timeline** + **Project Portfolio** + **AI Interview Score**.
- Synthesizes findings into a single Recruiter Executive Summary.
- Highlights key candidate strengths, risk factors, and custom interview talking points.

---

## 05. Jobs Module

Comprehensive job discovery and management system:
- **Job Search & Advanced Filtering:** Filter by title, technical skills, location, employment type, and match score threshold.
- **Recommended Jobs:** Intelligent AI recommendation feed personalized for each candidate.
- **Job Details View:** Deep-dive into job responsibilities, required skills, benefits, and company context.
- **Apply Workflow:** Streamlined application submission with instant resume matching analysis.
- **Save Jobs:** Bookmarking mechanism for candidates to track opportunities.
- **Match Score Badge:** Real-time score indicator displayed directly on job cards and listings.

---

## 06. Recruiter Workspace

Command center for talent acquisition professionals:
- **Job Requisition Creation & Editing:** Flexible job posting builder with skill tagging and weighting options.
- **Candidate Discovery & Search:** Search candidate database by skills, match scores, and experience tiers.
- **Candidate Ranking:** Instant sorting of applicants by total Resume Match Score and AI evaluation results.
- **Resume Matching Utility:** Standalone utility to upload job descriptions and candidate resumes for instant matching.
- **Interview Orchestration:** Schedule, manage, and review AI interview sessions.
- **Shortlisting & Pipeline Management:** Drag-and-drop or status-based candidate progress (Applied, Matched, Interviewed, Shortlisted, Hired, Rejected).
- **Hiring Analytics Dashboard:** High-level metrics on application volume, conversion rates, and recruitment velocity.

---

## 07. Candidate Workspace

Empowering portal for job seekers:
- **Profile Management:** Edit personal details, headline, summary, links, and work history.
- **Resume Manager:** Upload, update, and manage resume documents.
- **Skills Matrix:** Dynamic skills inventory with proficiency self-assessments and verified badges.
- **Match Score Insights:** View match score breakdowns across applied and saved job opportunities.
- **Recommended Jobs Feed:** AI-curated job recommendations updated dynamically based on profile improvements.
- **Application Tracker:** Real-time visibility into application status and recruiter actions.
- **Interview Portal:** Access AI interview invitations, practice workouts, and interview feedback summaries.
- **Saved Jobs:** Quick access to saved job postings.
- **Settings & Preferences:** Account preferences, notification toggles, and security settings.

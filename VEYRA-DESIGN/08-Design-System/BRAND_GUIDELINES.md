# VEYRA — Brand Guidelines & Identity System

## 1. Brand Identity Overview
- **Brand Name:** VEYRA
- **Product Category:** AI-powered Talent Intelligence Platform
- **Tagline:** See potential. Hire with confidence.
- **Brand Promise:** Empower recruiters with explainable talent insights while giving job seekers clear, actionable feedback on their career potential.

## 3. Conceptual Logo Direction
- **Wordmark:** Custom geometry using medium-weight typography with precise letter-spacing.
- **Symbol Concept:** Interlocking nodes symbolizing **Talent + Potential + Alignment**.
- **Usage & Clear Space:** Minimum clear space equal to the height of the letter 'V'.
- **Color Context:** Dark Navy (`#0F172A`) wordmark on light background; White (`#FFFFFF`) on dark surfaces.
- **Favicon:** Simplified monogram icon rendered in primary VEYRA Blue (`#1E40AF`).
